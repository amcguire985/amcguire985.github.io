Horse racing start gate sensor

Sep 2022 – Jan 2023 · CDP spin-out

Sole mechanical engineer for a CDP spin-out company. Developed a retrofittable sensor system to accurately detect race start time on existing racing infrastructure. From research and concepting through prototype manufacture and field validation.

[Placeholder: Site survey photos]

Field research at racing facility. Examined existing gate mechanisms, environmental conditions, and installation constraints. Identified vibration environment and temperature ranges.

[Placeholder: Mounting bracket design]

Aluminum mounting bracket designed for rapid retrofit. Accommodates ±3mm installation tolerance and uses quick-connect electrical connector for technician simplicity.

[Placeholder: Field installation]

Prototype installed on racing gate. Sensor housing rated for -10 to +50°C and IP67 (water resistance during gate cleaning).

[Placeholder: Data validation]

Timing validation data from field trials. Achieved ±10 ms accuracy across 50 race start events. Data logged and analyzed to establish performance baseline for regulatory submissions.

My role

Full ownership of mechanical design, requirements management, and prototype validation for this open-ended consulting brief. Conducted field research at racing facilities to understand gate mechanics and existing infrastructure constraints. Specified sensor placement, mounting bracket design, and weatherproofing requirements. Owned BoM, work instructions, and assembly documentation for technician handover. Coordinated with firmware team on sensor signal conditioning and timing accuracy validation.

Key technical achievements

Reduced installation time to under 30 minutes per gate through bracket design and quick-connect interfaces. Met ±10 ms timing accuracy requirement (critical for racing sport integrity). Achieved IP67 rating with conformal coating and mechanical seals — no housing degradation in 6-month field trial. Created assembly documentation and test procedures enabling technician deployment. Spin-out company successfully pitched to racing authority for nationwide rollout.
