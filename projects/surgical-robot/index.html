Prototype surgical robot

Sep 2023 – Mar 2024 · CDP

Redesign of a surgical robot patient cart to reduce mass and footprint allowing for more manouvreability within hospital. The cart needed to manipulate the instrument control unit (ICU) into various positions for different procedures. The system allowed surgeons to perform more complex endoluminal surgeries, reducing risk to patients by avoiding incisions.

Primary lift mechanism featuring ball screw actuator matched with stepper motor and magnetic brake. Designed for precise unloaded descent and fail-safe halt on power loss.

My role

A client came to us looking for help developing the next iteration of their surgical robot. The patient cart was too heavy to be pushed by hand, so required a motor and battery to move itself. This added further mass and size, leading to issues in getting the robot between operating theatres in a hospital. We proposed a redesign of the cart to reduce mass and footprint, enabling manual movement without motor assistance.
I owned the mechanical design of the primary lift mechanism, which was responsible for the vertical motion of the instrument control unit (ICU). I started by writing subsystem requirements based on the system technical requirements. I then used hand calculations and basic CAD models to specify the motor, brake, linear rails, and ball screw components. These long-lead time items needed to be ordered early in the project, so it was critical to de-risk them. I designed and built a proof-of-principle test rig to validate their performance under worst-case loading conditions.
I then moved on to detailed design of the CNC-machined aluminium sections that made up the structure of the vertical joint. We only had time for a single iteration for these parts, so I relied on hand calculations, FEA, and planned mitigations to ensure they were stiff enough to meet the system technical requirements.
Finally, I assembled the prototype. I made precise modifications to the parts as required, tuned the linear rails and belt drive, and worked with the hardware team to integrate the electronics into the assembly.

Key technical challenges

Successfully delivered a finished prototype on time that met requirements. Client filed for an application for a patent on which I was listed as an inventor. Generated key learnings for the next design iteration, communicated to client via in-depth presentation.
