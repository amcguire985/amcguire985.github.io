Novel diagnostics instrument

Jun 2024 – Feb 2025 · CDP

The client had developed an incubator to replicate cancer-fighting CAR-T cells specific to individual patients. Before treating the patient with the cells, a sample from the incubator needed to be tested for foreign cell contamination. This was previously done as a manual lab process taking multiple days. By automating the process, results could be obtained within a few hours. This dramatically improved the patient experience by reducing overall treatment time.

My role

Owned mechanical design and validation for the cartridge interface and actuation mechanism. Built multiple exploratory rigs to test fluid channel layouts, actuation force requirements, and material compatibility. Facilitated a 20-person client workshop to synthesize requirements from lab staff, clinicians, and device engineers. Coordinated rapid prototype iterations (2–3 per week) using 3D printing and laser welding. Drove risk-register-driven design, focusing on highest-impact failure modes first.

Key technical achievements

Reduced design cycle time through concurrent prototyping and validation. Identified and resolved critical failure mode: thermal stratification in cartridge, solved with inlet baffle design. Established repeatable test protocol for actuation force acceptance criteria. Managed material selection for biocompatibility and manufacturability (balance between 3D printing, injection moulding, and welding).
