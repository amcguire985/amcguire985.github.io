Child-resistant nicotine pouch container

Apr – May 2024 · CDP

The client approached us looking for a fresh perspective on some novel design requirements. They wanted a container that couldn't be accessed by children, but was still readily usable by dexterity-impaired adults. The project scope was to generate concepts for review, then develop approved concepts into injection-mouldable designs.

[Placeholder: Concept 1 CAD render]

Concept 1: Push-turn locking mechanism. Combines audible/tactile feedback with barrier to inadvertent opening. Patent filed on locking feature.

My role

I was the lead designer for this user-centred product development project. I planned and facilitated ideation sessions with cross-functional groups (client, design engineers, and human factors engineers). I led the downselection of concepts using a multi-criteria decision matrix. After initially planning to develop two concepts, the client extended the project to three. I owned the design for injection moulding across all three final designs.

Key technical achievements

Impressed client with three novel designs that met all requirements for complexity, cost, and function. All three were carried forward to patent applications.
