Mechanical infusion pump

Mar – Sep 2023 · CDP

The client wanted an update to a key product in their product line - a completely mechanical at-home infusion pump. This was a fantastic product because of its simplicity; the lack of electronics made regulatory approval straightforward and inexpensive. However, the product had an outdated look and clunky feel, and the client wanted us to investigate improving the user experience through novel actuation mechanisms. The product was mainly used by dexterity-impaired patients in their own home, significantly restricting the design space.

My role

Led mechanical design of one pump design variant from concepting through to finished prototype. Developed a rapid design iteration process using daily peer-to-peer design reviews and weekly SLA prints to quickly develop the mechanism and usability of the design. Owned robust CAD setup that allowed for quick changes late in the design process. Developed a simple and intuitive mechanism using a constant-force spring, rotary damper, and rack and pinion. Coordinated with human factors engineers and clinical teams to validate design against user and safety requirements.

Key technical achievements

Produced a design meeting all requirements within the project timeline. Of the three designs produced as part of the project, my design was chosen by the client to develop further into a works-like prototype. This directly led to an additional $1m project for the client developing the design further.
