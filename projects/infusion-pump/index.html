Mechanical infusion pump

Mar – Sep 2023 · CDP

Development of used-like prototypes of infusion pump designs for clinical user studies. Subsequent demonstrator built for investor trade shows and regulatory submissions. End-to-end ownership from concept through to finished demonstration hardware.

My role

Led mechanical design and rapid prototyping of one pump design variant through user study phase. Developed design iteration process: sketch → FDM print → team mini design review → next iteration. Achieved 2–4 print cycles per day using in-house 3D printing lab. Owned CAD model from sketch to finished prototype, including fluid pathway design, ergonomic handle sizing, and mechanical actuation feedback. Coordinated with regulatory and clinical teams to validate design against user workflows and safety requirements.

Key technical achievements

Achieved delivery accuracy of ±2% across 20–250 mL/hr range through mechanical gearing design and tight tolerance control. Rapid iteration methodology enabled 30 design variants tested within 6 months. User feedback integration led to haptic feedback improvement (button detent redesign) that reduced user delivery errors by 18% in usability study. All designs manufacturability-ready for injection moulding pilot production.
